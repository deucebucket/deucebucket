# Hey, I’m Jerry.

Online I usually build as **DeuceBucket**.

I build weird little AI systems and learn in public.

I do not come from the traditional computer science path. I work from first principles. Most of my ideas start as my own questions, hunches, or frustrations. I use AI as a thinking partner, challenger, and build assistant, then try to turn the useful pieces into something testable: a prototype, a workflow, a spec, a runbook, a failed experiment, or a better question.

Some of my repos are rough. Some are dead ends. Some are stepping stones. I keep the trail because the trail shows how I think.

## What I’m usually thinking about

- AI workflows that are useful instead of flashy
- Agent memory, state, feedback loops, and behavior boundaries
- Human-in-the-loop review instead of pretending automation is perfect
- Local/self-hosted AI tools, privacy, and practical infrastructure
- Book/library metadata, search, audio identification, and personal knowledge systems
- Model artifacts, GGUF experiments, local inference, and benchmark notes
- Asking “why?” when AI says something cannot be done

## How I work

I usually start messy, then try to make the mess explainable.

1. Break the problem down from first principles.
2. Ask why the default answer is the default answer.
3. Use AI to challenge, explore, and build possible structures.
4. Turn the useful parts into docs, prompts, tests, model artifacts, or small tools.
5. Look for where the idea breaks.
6. Keep the parts that survive contact with reality.

I am not trying to pretend I am a traditional senior engineer. I am learning by building. My strength is noticing weird patterns, asking better questions, and using AI to make those questions concrete enough to test.
